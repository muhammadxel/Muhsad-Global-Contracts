About Our Enterprise

Delivering Projects that Meet Agreed Standards and Expectations

MUHSAD GLOBAL CONTRACTS LIMITED is an indigenous Nigerian limited liability company incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act. We operate across civil engineering, building development, project planning, renovation, and logistics services.

Our operations are guided by a commitment to quality workmanship, strict cost discipline, responsible resource coordination, and sustainable value creation for private and public sector partners.
